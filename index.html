What’s in a Ship pack

  1. See which payment paths work

    We probe named facilitators, chains, and payload shapes against your live 402 route — so you see who actually verifies and settles, not what the SDK sample claimed.

  2. Find the cause of failed payments

    Identify rejected requests, mismatched header versions and undocumented format rules. Get a short list of fixes for your endpoint.

  3. Retest after changes

    Facilitators, chains, and header versions move. Optional retainer re-probes the matrix so last month’s green cell does not become this month’s silent 402.

Who for

Operators of paid APIs and agent wallets with an endpoint ready to test.

Who not

Teams still exploring payment ideas without an endpoint to test.
